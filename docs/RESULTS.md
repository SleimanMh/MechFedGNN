## 0. Summary

1. **Score-based donor weighting made no practical difference against equal
   donor weights.** In E1, all 48 score-arm vs `uniform-donor` contrasts have
   their whole 95 % interval inside ±2 % relative RMSE (widest bound 1.44 %;
   every point estimate within ±0.40 %). In E1M, where marginal rates are flat
   by design and only joint-missingness structure distinguishes clients, all
   72 contrasts are inside ±2 % as well.
2. **Collaborating at all matters, in both directions.** On wine every mixing
   arm is 6.6–7.5 % better than `local-only` (meaningful). On kin8nm and
   protein every mixing arm harms 3/4 and 4/4 receivers — small harms, below δ
   except `fedavg` on kin8nm, whose interval straddles it. No weighting rule
   avoided that harm.
3. **Choosing one donor can matter, but only with an oracle.** The test-picked
   best single donor beats the uniform mixture by up to 3–4 % on concrete; the
   validation-picked donor never does. This holds equally in E1M's null
   condition — it is about the estimability of donor usefulness, not about
   the scores.
4. **Why weighting did not matter (E1M geometry).** Clients' trained
   parameters are *not* close (pairwise distances 0.67–1.23× each client's
   distance from `θ0`), so "the clients are all alike" is not supported. But
   every score-weighted mixture lands within 0.00–0.34 of the uniform
   mixture, while single-donor mixes sit 0.50–1.72 from the centroid:
   averaging with weights in the range these scores produce barely moves the
   model.
5. **Group recovery cannot support the pairwise-structure claim in E1**:
   marginal-rate similarity alone recovers the injected groups perfectly
   (ARI 1.0 on all four datasets); the §10 prediction that `s` would do better
   failed.

## 2. Construction and pilots

**Masking conditions (rate 0.3, 10 seeds).** Expected per-feature rate 0.3000
everywhere; realised means 0.294–0.307. Within-panel φ ≈ 0.83 for panel
masking, ≈ 0 for cell masking; cross-panel φ ≈ 0 for independent panels and
0.13–0.17 when coupled. Cell masking has lift ≈ 1.00: joint absence at chance
(≈ 0.09), not zero. Null, dose-response and rate-calibration checks pass on
all four datasets (section 8).

**Budget pilot (3 seeds, validation fold).** No budget met the fixed rule
(spread ≥ 0.01 and stability ≥ 0.6) on every dataset — concrete's stability
peaked at 0.51, kin8nm and protein spreads stayed below 0.01. The fallback
chose 10 (tied with 25 on minimum stability 0.509).

**Power pilot.** At 10 seeds the predicted half-width is ≤ δ/2 for 21/21
contrasts on wine, kin8nm and protein, and 13/21 on concrete (its contrasts
against `local-only` were underpowered — confirmed in E1).

**Headroom pilot.** Local and pooled references early-stopped on the same
validation rule (protein up to 20,000 steps). POOLING HARMS in 0/48
receiver-seeds; concrete mostly HEADROOM OK, the others THIN or NO. Headroom
is reported as context only; it never excluded anything.

[+0.14, +0.29] at t2. At δ = 0.5 % the equivalence still holds on wine,

by the equal-donor control and do not establish an advantage from
score-based weighting.

**Validation-selected single donor vs `local-only`:** wine −6.02 %
(meaningful), concrete −3.70 % (meaningful), kin8nm −0.13 % and protein
−0.06 % (negligible; it picked `local-only` in 21 and 23 of 40
receiver-seeds).

**Score ordering vs measured transfer U** (top donor = top-U donor, of 40;
chance ≈ 13): `rate` 31 (kin8nm), 37 (protein), 18 (wine), 21 (concrete);
`s` 31 on kin8nm; coverage scores 6–20, with negative Kendall τ on kin8nm,
wine and protein.

**Group recovery (ARI vs true groups):** `rate` 1.0 on all four; `s` 1.0
(kin8nm), 0.8 (wine), 0.3 (protein), −0.05 (concrete); `S` at chance.
Singleton check: the group signal in `C` is the magnitude of within-panel φ
(≈ 0.94 vs 0.65, itself rate-driven), which cosine similarity discards when
only one within-panel block exists (concrete, protein).

**Ranking vs weighting (exploratory):** `s` and `S` scores barely vary across
donors (relative range ≤ 0.035) so their weights are ≈ uniform; `rate`, `W_H`
and `W_C` give clearly non-uniform weights (max 0.42–0.56) and still land
within ±0.4 % of uniform — the normalisation-compression hypothesis is
falsified. On kin8nm and protein the top-ranked donor is often the
least-harmful rather than a helpful one (40 % / 52 % of receiver-seeds for
`rate`'s top donor). Differences at t1 shrink after adaptation.

**Compute:** `local-only` ≈ 310 steps / 19.8k examples per receiver-seed;
every mixing arm ≈ 1,210 steps / 77.4k (it includes the donors' local
training) — about 4× the compute for no gain over `uniform-donor`.

## Limitations

- Masks are controlled synthetic constructions; no real missingness pattern
  was used or checked.
- One predictor (mask-aware MLP), one round, K = 4, γ = 0.5 fixed, β = 1 (no
  sharpening, by design).
- The adaptation budget (10) was chosen by the fallback rule and is weakly
  supported; concrete was underpowered against `local-only`.
- E1M's geometry is not E1's (E1 did not log parameters); L2 distance in
  parameter space is not loss.
- E1M on wine is confounded by feature relatedness (correlation-matching
  |diff| 0.198).
- Sections 6 and the E1 ranking/singleton analyses are exploratory
  (post hoc).
